# AI answers

When someone asks ChatGPT or Claude for "the best privacy-friendly analytics tool", are you in the answer? **AI answers** (beta) on the **Bots & AI crawlers** page checks that every day for the questions you choose.

It complements [AI crawlers and bots](/tracking/ai-crawlers), which shows what AI crawlers read on your site, and the AI referrals channel, which shows the people AI answers send you.

## How it works

1. You enter your brand (the names people use for you), your domains and up to 10 competitors.
2. You add up to 10 prompts: the questions your customers would type into an assistant.
3. Once a day (05:30 UTC), FlarePanel asks every prompt to each assistant you picked, with web search on, the way a person would:
   - **ChatGPT**: OpenAI's Responses API, `gpt-5.4-mini` with web search.
   - **Claude**: Anthropic's Messages API, `claude-haiku-5-5` with web search (up to 3 searches an answer).
4. For each answer it records:
   - **Named**: whether the answer names your brand or one of your domains (whole words, any case).
   - **Rank**: your place among the brands the answer names, by first mention. If it names two competitors before you, you are #3.
   - **Your site**: whether one of your domains is among the sources the answer cites or its searches read.
   - The competitors it names, the sources it used and the opening of the answer around your name.

**Check now** runs the same check on demand, at most once an hour.

## Your own AI key

Answers run on your workspace's [own OpenAI or Anthropic key](/features/ai-keys) (**Workspace settings → AI provider key**), so your provider bills the calls directly and FlarePanel never pays for or resells them. Each daily check makes one call per prompt and assistant: 10 prompts on both assistants is 20 calls a day. Web search is billed by the provider on top of tokens; check your provider's pricing.

If the workspace has no key, nothing runs and the card says so. If a call fails (an invalid key, a rate limit, web search turned off for your Anthropic organization), that answer shows **Failed** with the provider's message and is left out of the numbers. Failed calls are not retried, so you are never billed twice.

## Reading the numbers

- **Answers naming you** is the share of the last 30 days' answers that name you.
- **Average rank when named** only counts answers that name you.
- **Answers using your site** is the share of answers with one of your domains among their sources.
- **Named in answers** compares how many answers name you and each competitor.

Assistants answer the same question differently from one run to the next, and differently for different people. Read the trend over days, not a single answer. These are API answers from small models with web search: close to what the apps show, not the same.

## What is stored

Your brand, domains, competitors and prompts, and for each prompt, assistant and day: the result above, up to 20 source URLs and an excerpt of the answer (about 360 characters). Answers are kept 90 days. Removing a prompt removes its answers.

## Self-hosting

The check uses the workspace key when there is one, and otherwise `OPENAI_API_KEY` or `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` from your server's environment. Runs are queued on the `gsc` queue, like Search Console and the Cloudflare import.
