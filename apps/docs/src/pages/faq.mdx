# FAQ

### Is FlarePanel ready to use?

Yes. The hosted service is open with a 30-day free trial, and you can self-host the same code. [What works today](/getting-started/status) lists each part and its limits.

### How is it related to OpenPanel?

FlarePanel is an independent, open-source fork of [OpenPanel](https://github.com/Openpanel-dev/openpanel), rebuilt to run on Cloudflare. It keeps the tracking protocol so OpenPanel's SDKs work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenPanel.

### Is it made by Cloudflare?

No. It runs on Cloudflare's developer platform but is not affiliated with or endorsed by Cloudflare.

### Where is my data?

On Cloudflare: account data in D1, events in a Durable Object per project, and the raw archive in R2. When you self-host, all of it is in your own Cloudflare account, or in ClickHouse and SQLite on your own server.

### What does it cost?

Self-hosting is free and open source: you pay Cloudflare for what you use on a Workers Paid plan, or for your own server. Hosted plans are priced by monthly events, from $1 for 5,000 events, with a 30-day free trial: see [pricing](https://flarepanel.dev/pricing).

### Does it use cookies?

No tracking cookies. The SDK keeps ids in memory only; the server derives a visitor's device id from a hash of the IP address and user agent with a salt that rotates daily, so a visitor cannot be followed from one day to the next. The browser's session storage is used only to hold revenue you queue with `pendingRevenue`. The dashboard itself uses a session cookie for signed-in users.

### What is the license?

The platform is AGPL-3.0 and the SDKs are MIT.
